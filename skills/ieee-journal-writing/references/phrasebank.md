# 句式库（按段落功能分类）

- 每类开头给出语料统计："短语 k/72" 指 72 篇研究论文中至少用过一次的篇数，"每篇中位数" 指每篇的使用次数。
- 例句是原文单句短引，"…" 为省略；括号内为 DOI 后缀（省略 `10.1109/`）。
- 用法：先确定这一段的功能，再挑一个句式改写成自己的内容。不要照抄原句，也不要在一篇里反复用同一个句式。

## 14. 结果的描述与解释
统计："Fig. N shows/illustrates/…" 58/72；"It is observed / we can see that" 67/72（每篇中位数 6）；"This is because" 39/72；
"As expected" 22/72；"outperform" 50/72（中位数 2）；强调副词（significantly 等）62/72（中位数 3）。
写法：一图一段，先说图展示什么，再说现象，然后解释原因，最后给出启示。
- 指图："Fig. 4(a) illustrates the WSR of different schemes with respect to the transmit power P_T when N=100."（twc.2020.2970061）
- 现象："It is observed that the average sum rate achieved by the proposed design quickly increased with the number of iterations and converged in about 15 iterations."（tcomm.2025.3541027）
- 原因："This is because the user farther away from the AP may be closer to the IRS and thus it is able to receive stronger reflected signal from the IRS."（twc.2019.2936025）
- 预期之中："As expected, the total delay increases as the number of stop points increases."（twc.2016.2531652）
- 反直觉："The reason behind this counterintuitive behavior is that, …"（tcomm.2022.3231893）
- 趋势反转："The trend in Fig. 3 is reversed in Fig. 4, where the EE performance is sketched."（twc.2019.2922609）
- 条件式结论："… running FL with PF outperforms RS and RR if the network is operating under a high … threshold, while RR is more preferable when the SINR threshold is low."（tcomm.2019.2944169）
- 编号结论："Finally, our numerical results reveal that: 1) …; and 2) …"（twc.2021.3118225）
